# M05 — Arena360 Owner

## Outcome

An owner can understand and govern multiple locations without entering
Arena360 Staff or exposing location-only operations.

## In scope

- New Arena360 Owner product and portfolio overview.
- Location health, revenue, utilization, cash exceptions, inventory risks, and
  device/Edge health.
- Central pricing templates with location overrides and simulation.
- Shared customer, membership, wallet, promotion, and staff-access policies.
- Consolidated reports, audit search/export, and delegated regional access.
- Configuration snapshot and effective-value inspection.

## UX validation

- Compare locations, investigate an exception, publish a scoped policy, grant
  regional access, and verify the resulting audit/history.

## Non-goals

- No AI recommendations, partner management, or customer booking UI.
- No direct hardware command without an explicit operational role and scope.

## Exit gate

Owner actions are correctly scoped and explain inheritance; aggregate data
reconciles to location data; regional access cannot escape assigned locations.
