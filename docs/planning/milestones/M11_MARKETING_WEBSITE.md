# M11 — Arena360 Marketing Website

## Outcome

Prospective venues and partners can understand Arena360, choose the right
product/deployment path, trust the platform, and complete a measurable next
step.

## In scope

- New public website and content system, independent of authenticated apps.
- Product pages for Business, Automation, Intelligence, Cloud, Local, Edge,
  Hub, Owner, Staff, Player Self-Service, and Station.
- Solutions for small cafés, gaming arenas, chains/franchises, and partners.
- Pricing and fair-use presentation aligned with M09 entitlements and policy.
- Security, reliability, privacy, deployment, support, and status entry points.
- Demo/sales/partner lead capture, routing, consent, attribution, and CRM-ready
  handoff contract.
- Search, structured metadata, performance, accessibility, analytics, and
  content-governance foundations.
- Safe sign-in links to Admin, Owner, Staff, and Player products as applicable.

## UX validation

- A small café identifies the correct tier and books a demo.
- A chain compares Cloud and Local deployment.
- An installer finds the partner path.
- An existing customer reaches sign-in, support, documentation, or status
  without entering a sales funnel.

## Non-goals

- No unsupported feature promises, dark patterns, marketplace, community, or
  authenticated operational workflows.
- No pricing copy that conflicts with enforced entitlements or fair-use limits.

## Exit gate

Content is product-approved and accessible, page-performance budgets pass,
lead and consent data reach the correct destination, analytics measure the
conversion funnel, and all product claims map to delivered or clearly labeled
planned capabilities.
