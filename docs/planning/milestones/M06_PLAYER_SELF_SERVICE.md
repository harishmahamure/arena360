# M06 — Arena360 Player Self-Service

## Outcome

A player can discover availability, reserve or check in, pay or use an
entitlement, and manage a visit with minimal staff help.

## In scope

- New mobile-first Arena360 Player Self-Service application with registration,
  login, profile, memberships, wallet, history, offers, and privacy controls.
- Location/station-type availability and reservation lifecycle.
- Deposit/payment, cancellation, no-show, grace, auto-release, and refund rules.
- QR check-in and secure handoff to Arena360 Station where applicable.
- In-session food ordering and status.
- Player notifications and support/escalation path.

## UX validation

- First visit, returning member, wallet recharge, reservation change, late
  arrival, unavailable station, payment pending, refund, and venue offline.

## Non-goals

- No social network, tournaments, streaming, or recommendation feed.
- No unattended flow without explicit fraud, safety, and support review.

## Exit gate

A controlled location completes end-to-end self-service visits with correct
payment/session/reservation reconciliation, accessible flows, and measurable
staff-intervention rate.
