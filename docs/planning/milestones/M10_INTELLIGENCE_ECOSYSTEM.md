# M10 — Intelligence and ecosystem foundations

## Outcome

Arena360 can produce trusted owner recommendations and expose controlled
integration/partner capabilities without weakening tenant or operational safety.

## In scope

- Analytics event definitions, quality checks, retention, lineage, and tenant-
  safe aggregates.
- Utilization, station profitability, cohort/retention, membership performance,
  and demand signals.
- Explainable pricing recommendations with confidence, backtest, bounds, owner
  approval, and measured outcome; never automatic by default.
- Versioned public API and signed webhooks with retries, idempotency, replay,
  secrets rotation, quotas, and audit.
- Thin partner portal for approved onboarding, installation status, support
  handoff, and commission visibility.

## Non-goals

- No AI chatbot, social network, marketplace, esports platform, or autonomous
  price control.
- No cross-customer benchmarking until privacy thresholds and consent are
  formally approved.

## Exit gate

Metrics reconcile to source transactions, recommendations include reproducible
evidence, no tenant data leaks through analytics or APIs, and the first partner
and webhook workflows pass operational support review.
