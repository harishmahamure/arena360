# M09 — SaaS, Local licensing, and abuse controls

## Outcome

Arena360 can sell, entitle, meter, support, and govern Cloud and Local products
without exposing the platform to unbounded usage.

## In scope

- Business, Automation, Intelligence, Local, and enterprise entitlement model.
- Subscription/billing-provider boundary and customer-visible plan management.
- Signed offline license files, location scope, maintenance eligibility, and
  update entitlement for Local deployments.
- Usage metering, record-creation quotas, request/realtime/upload/automation
  limits, warnings, restrictions, appeal, overrides, and audit.
- Published fair-use and abuse policy aligned with product enforcement.
- Backup/restore, update channels, support bundle, health, and operational
  runbooks for Cloud, managed Local, and customer-managed Local.
- Cost telemetry against per-location COGS targets.

## Non-goals

- No device-based surprise pricing when the commercial promise is unlimited
  devices within fair use.
- No silent deletion or irreversible restriction when a quota is reached.

## Exit gate

Entitlement and quota tests cover upgrade, downgrade, expiry, grace, overage,
offline licensing, and abuse cases; policy text matches behavior; support can
explain and override enforcement safely.
