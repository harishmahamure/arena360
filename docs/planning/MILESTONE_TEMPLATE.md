# Mxx — Milestone name

## Outcome

Describe the measurable user or business result, not the implementation.

## Entry gate

- List required decisions, contracts, evidence, and predecessor outcomes.

## In scope

- Keep the slice small enough to validate independently.

## UX and operational states

- Cover happy path, empty, loading, stale, offline, denied, failure, recovery,
  and support behavior as applicable.

## Deliverables

- Link contracts, prototypes, migration plans, runbooks, and measurement plans.

## Non-goals

- State nearby work that is intentionally deferred.

## Risks and decisions

- Link decision-log and risk-register entries.

## Exit gate

- Define observable evidence required to mark the milestone done.
